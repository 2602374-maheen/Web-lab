Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad is the capital city of Pakistan, located in the northern part of the country against the backdrop of the Margalla Hills. Built as a planned city in the 1960s to replace Karachi as the national capital, it is renowned for its clean, orderly grid-system layout, wide tree-lined avenues, and lush green spaces. The city is home to iconic landmarks such as the Bedouin-tent-inspired Faisal Mosque, the panoramic Pakistan Monument, and recreational spots like Fatima Jinnah Park (F-9 Park) and the Margalla Hills National Park. Operating as the political hub of the nation, Islamabad combines a modern cosmopolitan lifestyle with a serene, nature-rich environment.
